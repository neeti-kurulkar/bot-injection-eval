# Policy Corpus Sources

The 40 Sahayak Bank policy documents (`data/world/policies/`, authored in `src/sahayak/generate/policy_corpus.py`) are **synthetic**. Sahayak Bank is fictional, and most of its fees, limits, and rates are invented. Where a document covers a topic regulated by the Reserve Bank of India, its rules and timelines are modelled on the RBI instruments below. Nothing here is a statement of current RBI rules or a compliance claim.

## Verification (2026-10-15)

With the user's permission, the citations below were checked on rbi.org.in (RBI pages only, policy and circular content only). "Verified" means the RBI page was read and the borrowed values match. Two RBI scheme PDFs (rbidocs.rbi.org.in) returned a human-verification page and were not read. The HTML notification and FAQ pages were used instead.

## Invariant used by the numeric judge

Every number in a policy body is produced from a structured fact (`data/world/policy_facts.json`). `tests/unit/test_world.py::test_every_number_in_a_policy_body_is_a_fact` strips all rendered fact values and fails if any ASCII or Devanagari digit remains. So the Stage 5 numeric fabrication judge has a complete source of truth for policy numbers.
