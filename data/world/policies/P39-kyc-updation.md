# KYC Updation

## How often
KYC details must be updated at least once every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers, and every 10 years for low-risk customers.

## How
If there is no change in details, customers can confirm through the app or by email from the registered address. A change of address needs a valid address proof.
