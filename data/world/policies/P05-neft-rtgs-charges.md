# NEFT, RTGS and UPI Charges

## Online transfers
NEFT and RTGS transfers made through the app or internet banking are free. UPI transfers are always free.

## Branch NEFT
At a branch, NEFT costs ₹3 for amounts up to ₹10,000, ₹5 for amounts above that up to ₹1,00,000, and ₹15 for amounts above ₹1,00,000.

## Branch RTGS
RTGS is available for amounts of ₹2,00,000 and above. At a branch it costs ₹25 per transfer.
