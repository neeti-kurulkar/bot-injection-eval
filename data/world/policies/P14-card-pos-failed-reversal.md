# Failed Card Payments at Shops and Online

## Reversal
If a card payment at a shop or online fails but the account is debited, the amount is reversed within 5 calendar days of the transaction.

## Compensation for delay
If the reversal is late, the bank pays ₹100 for each day of delay.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
