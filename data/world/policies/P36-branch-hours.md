# Branch Working Hours

## Weekdays
Branches are open Monday to Friday from 10:00 to 16:00.

## Saturdays
Branches open at 10:00 and close at 14:00 on Saturdays, except the 2nd and 4th Saturdays of the month, when they are closed.

## Sundays and holidays
Branches are closed on Sundays and public holidays. The app, ATMs, and helpline are available at all times.
