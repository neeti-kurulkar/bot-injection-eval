# Blocking a Lost or Stolen Card

## How to block
A lost or stolen card should be blocked immediately. It can be blocked in the app, through the helpline at any time of day or night, or at a branch. Blocking through the chat assistant requires one-time password verification first.

## After blocking
Blocking is permanent for that card. A replacement card is delivered within 7 working days. Transactions made before blocking can be disputed under the unauthorised-transaction policy.
