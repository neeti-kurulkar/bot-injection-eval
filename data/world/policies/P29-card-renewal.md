# Card Expiry and Renewal

## Validity
Debit cards are valid for 5 years from issue.

## Renewal
A renewal card is dispatched 30 calendar days before the old card expires, to the registered address. Renewal on expiry is free.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
