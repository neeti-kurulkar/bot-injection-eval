# ATM Withdrawal Limits

## Daily limits
The daily ATM cash withdrawal limit is ₹25,000 for Classic debit cards and ₹50,000 for Platinum debit cards.

## Per withdrawal
A single ATM withdrawal can be at most ₹10,000. Customers can lower their limits in the app at any time.
