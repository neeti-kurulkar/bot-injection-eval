# Fixed Deposits

## Tenure and amount
Fixed deposits can be opened for any tenure from 7 calendar days to 10 years, with a minimum amount of ₹1,000.

## Premature withdrawal
If a deposit is withdrawn early, interest is paid at the rate for the period actually held, minus 1%.
