# Savings Account Interest

## Rates
Savings accounts earn 2.75% a year on the part of the daily balance up to ₹1,00,000, and 3% a year on the part above ₹1,00,000.

## Credit
Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited at the end of each quarter.
