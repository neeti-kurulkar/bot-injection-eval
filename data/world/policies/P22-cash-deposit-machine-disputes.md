# Cash Deposit Machine Disputes

## Raising a dispute
If cash deposited at a cash deposit machine is not credited, the customer can raise a dispute within 30 calendar days of the deposit.

## Resolution
The bank reconciles the machine and resolves the dispute within 7 working days.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
