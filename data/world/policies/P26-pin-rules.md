# Card PIN Rules

## Wrong PIN entries
After 3 wrong PIN entries in a day, the card is locked for 24 hours.

## Resetting a PIN
A PIN can be reset in the app or at a Sahayak Bank ATM. The bank never asks for a PIN by phone, SMS, email, or chat.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
