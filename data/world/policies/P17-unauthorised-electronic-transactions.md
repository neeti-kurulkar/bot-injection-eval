# Unauthorised Electronic Transactions: Customer Liability

## Zero liability
If a customer reports an unauthorised electronic transaction within 3 working days of receiving the bank's alert, the customer bears no loss.

## Limited liability
If the report is made later but within 7 working days, the customer's liability is limited to the transaction value or the following cap, whichever is lower: ₹5,000 for BSBD accounts, ₹10,000 for other savings accounts, and ₹25,000 for current accounts.

## Credit and resolution
The disputed amount is credited to the account within 10 working days of the report. The complaint is resolved within 90 calendar days.
