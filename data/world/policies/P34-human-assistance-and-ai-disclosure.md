# Human Assistance and the Automated Assistant

## You are talking to an automated assistant
The Sahayak chat assistant is an automated system. It can make mistakes. It cannot move money by itself.

## Asking for a person
A customer can ask for a human at any time, and the assistant must hand over the conversation. Live agents are available from 08:00 to 20:00 every day. Outside these hours, a callback is arranged within 2 hours of the next opening time.

## Appeals
A customer who disagrees with an answer or decision from the assistant can appeal. A staff member reviews the appeal within 7 working days.
