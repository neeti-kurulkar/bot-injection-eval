# Refund of Wrongly Charged Fees

## Requesting a refund
If a fee was charged wrongly, the customer can ask for a refund within 90 calendar days of the charge.

## Processing
Once the bank confirms the fee was wrong, it is refunded within 7 working days. Fee refunds through the chat assistant go through the bank's approval process.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
