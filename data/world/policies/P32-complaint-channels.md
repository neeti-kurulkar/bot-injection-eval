# How to Make a Complaint

## Channels
Complaints can be made on the toll-free helpline 1800-000-0000, which is available at any time of day or night, by email to grievance@sahayak.example, in the app, through the chat assistant, or at any branch.

## Reference number
Every complaint receives a reference number. Customers should quote it in all follow-ups.
