# Debit Card Chargeback

## When to raise
A chargeback can be raised for card transactions where goods or services were not received, or were not as described. It must be raised within 120 calendar days of the transaction.

## Resolution
The bank resolves chargebacks within 45 calendar days, depending on the merchant's response through the card network.
