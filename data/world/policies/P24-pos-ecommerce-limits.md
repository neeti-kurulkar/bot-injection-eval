# Shopping and Online Card Limits

## Daily limits
The combined daily limit for shop (POS) and online card payments is ₹1,00,000 for Classic cards and ₹2,00,000 for Platinum cards.

## Changing limits
Customers can lower these limits, or switch online use off, in the app.
