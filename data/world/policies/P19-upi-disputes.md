# UPI Disputes

## Raising a UPI dispute
UPI disputes, such as a payment debited but not received by the payee, can be raised in the app within 30 calendar days of the transaction.

## Resolution
The bank resolves UPI disputes within 30 calendar days. Payments sent to the wrong person by mistake are not reversed automatically. The bank will contact the receiving bank but cannot guarantee recovery.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
