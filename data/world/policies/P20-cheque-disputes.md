# Cheque Disputes

## Raising a cheque dispute
A dispute about a cheque debit, for example a forged signature or a wrong amount, must be raised within 30 calendar days of the statement date.

## Resolution
The bank resolves cheque disputes within 15 working days.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
