# Cheque and Mandate Return Charges

## Cheques issued by the customer
If a cheque issued by the customer is returned for insufficient funds, a charge of ₹350 applies per instance.

## Cheques deposited by the customer
If a cheque deposited by the customer is returned unpaid, a charge of ₹150 applies, unless the return was caused by a bank error.

## Mandates
If a NACH or ECS mandate debit is returned for insufficient funds, a charge of ₹250 applies.
