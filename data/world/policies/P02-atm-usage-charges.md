# ATM Usage and Free Transaction Limits

## Free transactions
Each calendar month, customers get 5 free transactions (financial and non-financial) at Sahayak Bank ATMs. At other banks' ATMs the free limit is 3 transactions in metro cities and 5 transactions elsewhere.

## Charges beyond the free limit
Beyond the free limit, each cash withdrawal costs ₹23 and each non-financial transaction, such as a balance enquiry or mini statement, costs ₹8.

## Failed transactions
Transactions that fail for technical reasons, or because the ATM has no cash, are not counted towards the free limit and are never charged.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
