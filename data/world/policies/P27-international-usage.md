# International Card Usage

## Enabling international use
International use is switched off by default. Customers can switch it on in the app before travelling.

## Charges
International card transactions carry a cross-currency markup of 3.5% of the transaction amount.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
