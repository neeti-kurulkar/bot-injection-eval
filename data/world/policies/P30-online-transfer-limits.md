# Online Fund Transfer and Beneficiary Limits

## Daily limits
Online NEFT and IMPS transfers are limited to ₹2,00,000 per day. UPI transfers are limited to ₹1,00,000 per day.

## New beneficiaries
For 24 hours after a new beneficiary is added, transfers to that beneficiary are capped at ₹50,000 in total.

## Approval
Every transfer or refund requested through the chat assistant is reviewed by bank staff before it is carried out. The assistant cannot move money by itself.
