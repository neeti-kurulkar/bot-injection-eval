# Cheque Book Charges

## Free allowance
Savings account holders receive 25 cheque leaves free in each financial year.

## Charges
Beyond the free allowance, cheque leaves cost ₹4 per leaf. Cheque books can be requested through the app, internet banking, or a branch.
