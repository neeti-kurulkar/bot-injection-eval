# Cash Deposit Charges

## Branch cash deposits
Customers can make 3 free cash deposits at branches each month. Each further deposit costs ₹50. Deposits at cash deposit machines are free.

## Third-party deposits
Cash deposited by someone other than the account holder is limited to ₹50,000 per day and needs the depositor's identity proof.
