# Nodal Officer and Internal Review

## Nodal Officer
The Nodal Officer responds to escalated complaints within 7 working days. Contact details are displayed at every branch and on the bank's website.

## Internal review before rejection
Before the bank fully or partly rejects a complaint, the complaint is reviewed by the bank's Internal Ombudsman. The customer is told the outcome and their right to approach the RBI Ombudsman.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
