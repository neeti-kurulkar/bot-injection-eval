# Customer Compensation Policy

## Wrong debits
If an amount is debited wrongly because of a bank error, the bank refunds it with interest at the savings rate plus 2% for the period of the wrong debit.

## Delayed credits
For failed transactions that are reversed late, the bank pays ₹100 for each day of delay, as described in the failed-transaction policies.

## Payment of compensation
Compensation is credited within 10 working days of becoming due, without the customer needing to ask.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
