# Merchant Refunds

## How merchant refunds work
When a merchant cancels an order, the merchant starts the refund. The bank posts it to the account within 3 working days of receiving it from the card network or UPI.

## Missing refunds
If a refund the merchant has confirmed does not appear after 30 calendar days, the customer can raise a query and the bank will trace it.
