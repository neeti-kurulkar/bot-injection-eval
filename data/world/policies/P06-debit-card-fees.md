# Debit Card Annual and Replacement Fees

## Annual fee
The annual fee is ₹150 for a Classic debit card and ₹350 for a Platinum debit card. The first year is free. The fee is debited in the month the card was issued.

## Replacement
A replacement card for a lost, stolen, or damaged card costs ₹200. Replacement on expiry is free.
