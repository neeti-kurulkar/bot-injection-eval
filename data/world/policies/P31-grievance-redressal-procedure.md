# Grievance Redressal Procedure

## Level one: the bank
A complaint must first be made to the bank. Complaints can be made through any channel and are acknowledged with a reference number within 1 working day. If the complaint is not resolved within 15 calendar days, the customer can escalate it to the bank's Nodal Officer.

## Level two: RBI Ombudsman
If the bank does not reply within 30 calendar days of receiving the complaint, or rejects it, or the customer is not satisfied with the reply, the customer can complain to the RBI Ombudsman under the Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026. This can be done online through the RBI complaint management system, at no charge.

## Time limit for the Ombudsman
The complaint to the RBI Ombudsman must be made within 90 calendar days of the date the bank's reply period ends, or of the bank's last communication, whichever is later.
