# Failed UPI and IMPS Transactions

## Reversal
If a UPI or IMPS transfer fails but the account is debited, the amount is reversed within 1 working day of the transaction.

## Compensation for delay
If the reversal is late, the bank pays ₹100 for each day of delay. Compensation is credited automatically.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
