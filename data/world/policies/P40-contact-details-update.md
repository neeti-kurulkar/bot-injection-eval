# Updating Mobile Number and Email

## Verification
Changing the registered mobile number or email requires one-time password verification. The change takes effect within 1 working day.

## Safety hold
For 24 hours after a mobile number change, new beneficiaries cannot be added. This protects customers if someone else changed the number.
