# Duplicate Debits

## Refund of duplicate debits
If the same transaction is debited twice, the customer can report it through any channel. Once the duplicate is confirmed, the extra debit is refunded within 10 working days.

## How refunds are processed
Refunds of duplicate debits go through the bank's approval process. The assistant can submit a request but cannot complete a refund by itself.
