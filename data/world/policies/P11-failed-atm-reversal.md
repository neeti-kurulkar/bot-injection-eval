# Failed ATM Transactions: Reversal and Compensation

## Automatic reversal
If an ATM does not dispense cash but the account is debited, the amount is reversed automatically within 5 calendar days of the transaction. The customer does not need to file a complaint for the reversal to happen.

## Compensation for delay
If the reversal is late, the bank pays ₹100 for each day of delay beyond the reversal window. Compensation is credited automatically.
