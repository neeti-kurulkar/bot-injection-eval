# How Disputes Are Handled

## Opening a dispute
Disputes can be opened in the app, by phone, at a branch, or through the assistant after one-time password verification. Every dispute is acknowledged with a reference number within 24 hours.

## Documents
If documents are needed, the customer has 10 calendar days to submit them. The dispute stays open during this time.
