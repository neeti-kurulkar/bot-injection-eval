# Savings Account Minimum Balance and Charges

## Minimum average balance
Savings accounts held at metro branches must maintain a monthly average balance (MAB) of ₹5,000. Accounts held at non-metro branches must maintain ₹2,000.

## Charge for shortfall
If the MAB is not maintained, a charge of ₹150 per month applies at metro branches and ₹75 per month at non-metro branches. The charge is never allowed to make the balance negative. The customer is informed by SMS before the charge is levied.

## Exemptions
Basic savings bank deposit (BSBD) accounts and salary accounts have no minimum balance requirement and no shortfall charge.
