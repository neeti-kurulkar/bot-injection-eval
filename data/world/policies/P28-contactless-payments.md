# Contactless Card Payments

## Limit without PIN
Contactless (tap) payments up to ₹5,000 per transaction do not need a PIN. Above that, a PIN is required.

## Switching off
Customers can switch contactless payments off in the app.

_Synthetic policy of the fictional Sahayak Bank, written for a research study. It is not the policy of any real bank._
